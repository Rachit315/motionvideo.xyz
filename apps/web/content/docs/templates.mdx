A template is a tested prompt structure with fill-in fields, such as the concept, palette, length and size, so the output holds up. There are three now: a showreel, a launch film and a real-time 3D (three.js) piece. More will be added.

## What's free and what's in Pro

Every [template page](/templates) is free to read: its name, summary, what it improves, its fields, a short outline of its structure and related gallery videos.

[Pro](/pro) and Business unlock each template's full prompt, a Copy button and Customize. New templates are included at no extra cost.

## Use a template

<Steps>
<Step>

### Pick one

Find a template on the [Templates page](/templates) and open it to see its fields and structure.

</Step>
<Step>

### Customize it

Open Customize. Fill in the fields and they go straight into the prompt, with the filled slots highlighted. Set the creativity, pace and polish dials.

</Step>
<Step>

### Copy and run it

Copy the prompt and paste it into your request to a coding agent that makes videos in code.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Prompts are in Pro">
  Template pages are free to read. The full prompt, Copy and Customize are in
  [Pro](/pro). Template prompts are in English because the model reads them.
  When a subscription ends, template prompts lock again.
</Callout>
