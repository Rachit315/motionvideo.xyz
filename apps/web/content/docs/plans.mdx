Pro and Business unlock the same things: every move's prompt, the Pro agent skill, the Pro MCP tools and every future update. Gallery videos and their prompts stay free, with or without a plan.

## Prices

| Plan     | Monthly | Annual | Lifetime    |
| -------- | ------- | ------ | ----------- |
| Pro      | $9      | $79    | $149 once   |
| Business | $29     | $249   | Not offered |

Prices are shown before you check out, and taxes are added where they apply. See [pricing](/pro).

## Pro and Business

Pro is for one person, paid monthly, annually or once for lifetime. Business is monthly or annual and includes 3 seats: yours and 2 teammates.

## Seats

Invite teammates by email from [Billing in your dashboard](/dashboard/billing). Each teammate signs in with the invited email and gets everything in Pro. Remove a seat anytime to give it to someone else.

## Pro MCP tools

The [Pro MCP tools](/docs/mcp#pro-tools) use your motionvideo.xyz account, so there's no key to copy. Your MCP client opens the sign-in the first time you use them. Sign in with the email on your plan, or as a Business teammate with the email you were invited with.

They work while your plan is active and stop when it ends. A lifetime plan never expires.

## Renewal and cancellation

Monthly and annual plans renew automatically until you cancel. To cancel or get receipts and invoices, open [Billing](/dashboard/billing) and go to the Dodo Payments customer portal. A canceled plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, and you are not charged again. There are no partial refunds for the rest of a period.

Lifetime is paid once and does not renew.

## Refunds

Dodo Payments, our merchant of record, processes every payment and refund. See the [refund policy](/refunds) for when a refund applies.
