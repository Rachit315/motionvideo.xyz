## Browse by colour

Every video now carries its dominant colours, and the gallery filter has a colour row. Colours with enough videos get a page of their own, like [blue](/color/blue), [purple](/color/purple) and [black](/color/black).

## Similar videos

Each video page ends with six videos like it, picked by shared categories, a similar palette, the same model and the same creator, so one good find leads to the next.

## Nine languages

MotionVideo now speaks English, Spanish, French, Chinese, Korean, Japanese, German, Portuguese and Russian. Every page has its own address per language, with translated titles and descriptions for search engines.

## Directories

- Filters and sorting on [Tools](/tools), [Creatives](/creatives), [Skills](/skills) and [Extras](/extras).
- 100 new video agent skills and 31 new motion graphics videos.
- A redesigned sign-in page.

## Under the hood

- Pages load faster and are cached at the edge for visitors who aren't signed in.
- Sharing a MotionVideo link on Slack, Discord, iMessage or LinkedIn now shows a short looping video.
- Duplicate videos were found and removed from the gallery.
